## 2. Full Description (Google Play & Apple App Store)

```text
Experience farm freshness and quality with TOHFA — your trusted marketplace for certified organic fruits, fresh vegetables, and regional staples.

CONSOLIDATED REGIONAL INVENTORY
• Browse high-grade agricultural produce sourced directly into certified regional warehouses.
• View transparent pricing per kilogram with clear quality grade badges and organic certification tags.
• Dynamic category filters: Seasonal Fruits, Farm Vegetables, Pulses, and Traditional Grains.

24-HOUR CART RESERVATION LOCK
• Lock in current stock availability for 24 hours while you build your order.
• Guaranteed allocation directly from warehouse inventory ensures no out-of-stock substitutions.

WALLET-FIRST SEAMLESS CHECKOUT
• Enjoy instant, 1-tap checkout powered by your secure in-app TOHFA Wallet.
• Fast UPI and Card top-up recovery via Razorpay with automated payment confirmation.
• Zero checkout friction and clear transparent itemized billing.

REAL-TIME ORDER FULFILMENT & TRACKING
• Real-time dispatch, transit, and delivery status updates via live Server-Sent Events (SSE).
• Secure contact-free handover with dynamic 4-digit verification OTP.
• Flexible delivery options: doorstep scheduled delivery or express regional warehouse pickup.

PRIVACY & ANONYMITY GUARANTEE (BR-16):
• The TOHFA Customer application is built on a strict privacy-first architecture. We only collect essential delivery details (mobile number, delivery address) and standard payment transaction identifiers.
• We NEVER request device location, camera, contacts, or sensitive hardware permissions.
• To protect supplier privacy and prevent supply chain distortion, our catalog operates on an anonymous aggregated warehouse inventory model.
```
