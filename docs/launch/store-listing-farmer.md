## 3. Prominent Disclosure Justifications (Play Store Policy)

### A. Aadhaar / KYC Identity Verification (Rule BR-33)
- **Purpose:** Verification of bona fide farmer identity for agricultural direct-trade compliance and direct-to-bank payout disbursements.
- **In-App Disclosure:** Displayed prior to step 2 of registration with explicit consent checkboxes. Number masked in logs and stored encrypted.

### B. Precise GPS Location Access
- **Purpose:** Geolocation tagging of crop harvest fields to calculate delivery distance to local warehouse hubs and prevent phantom listings.
- **In-App Disclosure:** Displayed when tapping "Capture Farm Location" with foreground-only execution.
